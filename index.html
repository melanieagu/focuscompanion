How the pact works
  • Only the wallet you invite can join. The creator can cancel before it joins.
  • The timer starts when your buddy’s matching deposit confirms onchain.
  • After the timer, both report completion. This relies on honesty; the app does not verify your work.
  • Both confirmations unlock both deposits. Claiming your deposit is a separate transaction.
  • If a buddy never joins, refund is available after 24 hours (or cancel sooner). If someone does not confirm, both deposits can be unlocked 10 minutes after the session ends.
  • No forfeitures or prizes. Testnet gas is separate and is not refunded.
